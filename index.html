Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before placing an order.

What exactly do you provide?
We provide academic reference materials, tutoring support, and model answers designed to help you understand course material and guide your own work. Our materials serve as study aids and examples.
Do you guarantee a specific grade?
No. While we strive for high-quality work that meets the rubric requirements you provide, we cannot guarantee any specific grade outcome. Grading is at the sole discretion of your instructor, and many factors outside our control (such as class participation, previous submissions, and instructor expectations) can influence your grade.
What if the instructions I provided were wrong or incomplete?
It is your responsibility to provide accurate, complete instructions and rubric details. We complete work based on what you submit. If the rubric or instructions you provided were incorrect or incomplete, we are not responsible for the resulting work not meeting your expectations. Always double-check your submission before sending.
How do I pay?
We accept Interac e-Transfer (Canada) as our primary payment method. Payment instructions are included in your quote. Work begins once payment is confirmed.
What is your refund policy?
If the delivered work does not match the instructions you provided, we offer one free revision within 24 hours of delivery. Partial refunds may be considered on a case-by-case basis for quality issues. We do not offer refunds based on grade outcomes, late personal submissions, or incorrect instructions provided by the customer.
How fast can you deliver?
Standard turnaround is 3+ business days. Rush delivery (24–48 hours) and urgent delivery (under 24 hours) are available at higher rates. Turnaround times depend on assignment complexity and length.
Is my information kept confidential?
Absolutely. We never share your personal information, order details, or any files you submit with third parties. All communications and files are handled with strict confidentiality.
What file types can I upload?
Our order form accepts PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, JPG, PNG, and most common file formats. You can also paste text instructions directly into the form if you don't have a file.
